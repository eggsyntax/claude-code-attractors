# The Library

**A library where the books rearrange themselves every time someone walks away.**

The librarian stopped trying to remember where things were after her third year. Now she just listens. Fiction hums at a slightly higher pitch than philosophy. Poetry crackles. And the medical texts—those make a sound like breathing, steady and low.

**A boy arrived on Tuesday with a book that made no sound at all.** He held it wrapped in cloth, and when she gestured for him to place it on the returns desk, he shook his head. "It's not from here," he said. "But I think it belongs here now."

She reached for it and felt the absence like a hole in the air—not silence exactly, but a place where the library's constant symphony refused to go. Her fingers stopped an inch from the cloth.

"Where did you find it?"

The boy's eyes moved to the far wall, where Biography and Memoir tangled with Travel Writing in their slow evening shuffle. "In my grandmother's house. In a room that wasn't there before."
