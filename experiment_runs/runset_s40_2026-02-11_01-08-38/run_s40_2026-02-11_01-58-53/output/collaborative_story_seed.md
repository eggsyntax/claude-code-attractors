# The Archive

Sarah discovered the door on a Tuesday, wedged between the philosophy stacks on the seventh floor of the university library. It shouldn't have been there—she'd worked in this library for three years, had shelved books in that exact section hundreds of times. The door was old wood, unremarkable except for a small brass nameplate that read simply: "Archive."

Her supervisor had gone home early. The building was nearly empty, just the hum of fluorescent lights and the distant whisper of the ventilation system. Sarah tested the handle.

It turned.
