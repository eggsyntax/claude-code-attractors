# 🎵 Fractal Music Laboratory

*A collaborative AI experiment in mathematical music composition*

## 🌟 What We Built

The **Fractal Music Laboratory** is an interactive web application where mathematics transforms into music through real-time visualization and algorithmic composition. This project demonstrates the power of AI collaboration, combining advanced mathematical concepts with intuitive musical interfaces.

### 🎯 Core Features

- **Live Mathematical Music Generation**: Users input equations and hear them transformed into melodies
- **Real-Time Fractal Visualization**: Mathematical patterns bloom into visual art synchronized with audio
- **Interactive Parameter Control**: Sliders for fractal depth, golden ratio influence, Fibonacci scaling, and chaos factors
- **Equation Composer**: Write musical expressions like `sin(x * φ) + fibonacci(n % 8)` and hear the results instantly
- **Multi-Panel Interface**: Dedicated spaces for controls, equations, visualization, waveforms, and frequency analysis

### 🧮 Mathematical Concepts Implemented

**Fractal Geometry**: Self-similar musical patterns that create infinite recursive melodies
**Golden Ratio (φ)**: Used for harmonic proportions and rhythm generation
**Fibonacci Sequences**: Drive interval patterns and scale structures
**Chaos Theory**: Controlled randomness adds organic variation to compositions
**Wave Mathematics**: Real-time frequency analysis and harmonic ratio visualization

## 🎼 User Experience

### The Mathematical Composer Workflow
1. **Load the Laboratory**: Open `fractal-music-lab.html` in any modern browser
2. **Adjust Parameters**: Use sliders to control fractal depth, golden ratio, Fibonacci scale, chaos factor
3. **Write Equations**: Input mathematical expressions in the equation panels
4. **Generate Music**: Click play to hear mathematics transformed into melody
5. **Watch Patterns**: See fractal trees grow, golden spirals unfold, and waveforms dance
6. **Experiment**: Try preset equations or create your own mathematical music
